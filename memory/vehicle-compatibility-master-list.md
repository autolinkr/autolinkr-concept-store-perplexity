# AutoLinkr — Master supported vehicle list (draft)

**Sources:** `memory/Compatibility.txt` — (1) AutoLinkr customer-supported list, (2) Apple CarPlay compatible vehicles export (lower section of that file).

**Important:** This is an internal working document. Customer-facing copy must still emphasize **trim-level CarPlay/Android Auto + data-capable USB**. Do not treat this as a warranty of compatibility.

---

## Rules applied

0. **No new makes in the master list:** The published AutoLinkr supported list keeps the **same manufacturer set** as today. We do **not** add new make sections (no Abarth, MG-only rows, etc.). Apple-only makes stay in **Appendix A** for internal reference only — **never** merged into the customer master. Optional **new models** are only considered **under makes we already list** (see “Models added from Apple list”).
1. **Years:** AutoLinkr ranges take priority. Where a model was still current through **2025** on Apple / in the prior list and is treated as an ongoing production vehicle, the **end year is advanced to 2026** (Rule 5). Models that **ended production** before 2025 keep their original end year.
2. **Special cases retained:** BMW **limited** list only (not Apple’s full BMW matrix). Ford **Explorer / Expedition 2017–2024** + Google infotainment note. GM EV warnings. Hyundai/Kia **dealership wired CarPlay** notes. Kia **Seltos** trim note. Cadillac Escalade **IQ** / **LYRIQ** caveats, etc.
3. **Integra:** Extended **(2023–2024) → (2023–2026)** to align with ongoing model years (Rule 1 / 5).
4. **Genesis:** Duplicate **G70** lines consolidated to **G70 (2020–2026)**.
5. **Hyundai:** Typo **Aple → Apple**; note years **2022–2025 → 2022–2026** where it refers to dealership-enablement window.
6. **Kia:** Same **2022–2026** window in the dealership note.
7. **Chevrolet:** All prior **–2025** **range ends** on still-sold models bumped to **–2026** (Rule 5); discontinued models unchanged. Footnote on **Trax** generalized (no longer tied only to “2025 trims”).
8. **Additions from Apple** (only under **existing** makes): listed in the next section with **`[Added from Apple list — verify]`**. These are candidates to add as **model lines**, not new brands.
9. **Appendix A:** Apple-export **make slugs** with **no** AutoLinkr section — **internal review / competitive reference only** — **do not add to master** (Rule 0).
