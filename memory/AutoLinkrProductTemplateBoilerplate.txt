================================================================================
  UPGRADING TO A LANDING PAGE
================================================================================

  When a product becomes a flagship and needs a full landing page experience,
  add sections ON TOP of this foundation. Pick from:

    • Feature strip ........... Trust badges (setup, shipping, warranty)
    • Product video ........... Demo or 3D showcase
    • Press logos .............. "As Featured In" credibility strip
    • Feature breakdown ........ Icon grid or image-with-text deep dive
    • Compatibility section .... Dedicated objection handler with CTA
    • How it works ............. 3-step visual walkthrough
    • Testimonials banner ...... 3 review cards with parallax
    • Product comparison ....... Side-by-side vs other products in the line
    • Cross-sell ............... Featured companion product
    • FAQ ...................... Hesitation-focused accordion (5-10 Qs)
    • Final CTA ............... Value stack + last push

  See AutoLinkrProPageDesign.txt for a complete example of this.
