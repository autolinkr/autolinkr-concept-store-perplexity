================================================================================
  AUTOLINKR™ — HOMEPAGE MASTER TEMPLATE
  Brand-First Storefront Blueprint

  PRINCIPLE: The homepage is NOT a product page. It's a brand storefront.
  Its job is to (1) establish who AutoLinkr is, (2) build trust fast,
  (3) tease the hero product enough to drive clicks, (4) surface the
  full product line, and (5) route visitors to the right product page.

  Everything detailed (features, specs, how-it-works, comparisons, FAQs)
  lives on the PRODUCT pages. The homepage should make visitors want to
  click through — not give them everything so they never need to.

  RULE: If it already exists on the Pro page, it doesn't belong here
  unless it serves a distinctly different purpose on the homepage.
================================================================================

================================================================================
  WHY THIS IS BETTER
================================================================================

  WHAT WE CUT:
    - 3 sections that repeated the same stats (scrolling text, number counter)
    - 1 feature breakdown that duplicates Pro page content (multicolumn-why)
    - 1 comparison table that duplicates Pro page content (product-comparison)
    - 1 standalone header that should be merged (dash-intro-header)

  WHAT WE GAIN:
    - Tighter scroll — visitors reach the bottom faster
    - No redundant content with product pages
    - Clear routing: Hero → Pro spotlight → Browse categories → Dash → Trust → Go
    - Each section has ONE unique job that no other section does
    - Product pages get to do what they do best (sell with detail)
    - Homepage gets to do what IT does best (build brand, route traffic)

  THE HOMEPAGE FUNNEL:
    "What is AutoLinkr?" (hero + feature strip + video) →
    "Show me the star product" (featured Pro) →
    "What else do they make?" (collection list + featured Dash) →
    "Can I trust them?" (testimonials + mission) →
    "I'm ready" (final CTA)

  EVERY CLICK FROM THE HOMEPAGE SHOULD LAND ON A PRODUCT PAGE.
  That's where the detailed selling happens — features, comparisons,
  FAQs, specs, reviews. The homepage is the appetizer, not the meal.
